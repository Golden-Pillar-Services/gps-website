# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: B2B decision-makers in Oman vetting a contractor before requesting
a quote — property owners, real-estate developers, retail/mall managers,
facility-management companies, and government procurement teams. They
shortlist several contractors, then verify credibility (Is this company
real? Established? Capable of the specific trade mix they need?) before
picking up the phone or opening WhatsApp. They read Arabic and/or English
natively; many read Arabic as their working language, not English-with-
translation.

## Product Purpose

Golden Pillar Services is an Omani contractor operating four trades: fitout
& construction, cleaning services, joinery, and signage. The website exists
to convert a shortlist visit into a WhatsApp/phone/email inquiry by
demonstrating real capability and trustworthiness — not to sell online or
collect leads through a form.

## Positioning

One accountable contractor across fitout, cleaning, joinery, and signage —
a client deals with a single point of contact and one contract instead of
coordinating four separate subcontractors. Backed by direct Omani market
knowledge (local approvals, local suppliers, local labour law).

## Operating Context

Client discovers the company via referral, tender shortlist, or search;
compares 2-4 contractor sites; contacts via WhatsApp (primary), phone, or
email — no online form, no self-serve checkout. Company is based in
Muscat and works project sites across Oman. Deals are B2B, relationship-
and reputation-driven, not impulse purchases.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site, no backend, no CMS, no contact form — WhatsApp/tel/mailto
  links only.
- Fully bilingual: English and Arabic, with genuine right-to-left layout
  for Arabic (not a mirrored afterthought).
- No e-commerce, no login, no dashboards.
- Current build: Astro + Tailwind v4. Framework/stack is not a locked
  constraint — free to change if a different visual world genuinely needs
  it — but a full rebuild is out of scope unless the chosen direction
  requires it.
- Real company photography, real project case studies, and final contact
  details (phone/email/address/CR number) do not exist yet — the current
  site uses realistic-but-fabricated placeholders (Unsplash-sourced stock
  photography of construction/joinery/cleaning/signage work, invented
  project names and stats). Any redesign must keep this placeholder
  status honest and swappable, not bury it.

## Brand Commitments

- Company name: Golden Pillar Services (Arabic: الركيزة الذهبية للخدمات).
- Existing logo mark (pillar/column motif) in gold (#C9932B) and charcoal
  (#343434) is a fixed brand asset — the mark itself is binding. The
  broader visual system built around it (fonts, layout, motion, how gold
  is used) is NOT binding and is explicitly open for full reimagining.
- No other locked brand assets (no existing photography, no prior website
  to preserve — the current Astro build is itself a first attempt the
  user has rejected as generic, not an incumbent identity to protect).

## Evidence on Hand

- Real: company name, logo SVG, service catalog (4 trades + their real
  scope items, e.g. commercial/retail/office fitout; deep/facade/post-
  construction cleaning; doors/wardrobes/kitchens joinery; 3D letters/
  lightbox/wayfinding signage), Oman/Muscat market context.
- Fabricated placeholders (must stay swappable, not hardcoded as fact):
  contact details, CR number, map location, company stats (years/
  projects/clients), 5 company-history milestones, 8 project case
  studies, all photography.

## Product Principles

1. Credibility over cleverness — every design decision should make a
   skeptical B2B buyer trust this company more, not just look impressive.
2. Arabic is a first-class language, not a mirrored English site — typography,
   rhythm, and RTL layout must be designed for it directly.
3. Show real trade craft — the four services are physical, skilled work;
   the site should feel like it was made by people who understand
   fitout/joinery/signage/cleaning, not a generic SaaS template reskinned.
4. Static-site honesty — no fake interactivity, dashboards, or app-like
   chrome that a contractor site has no business having.
5. The rebuild has full creative license on the visual system (palette
   usage, type, layout, motion) as long as the logo mark and bilingual
   RTL requirement survive.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA minimum (contrast, focus states, alt text in both languages,
touch targets ≥44px) — no explicit user-reported accessibility need beyond
standard compliance.
